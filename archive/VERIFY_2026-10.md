(above httpd) and a failed task start ends the call instead of connecting
it with one-way audio. The SIP task already exits when VoIP is off.

dialup.mp3, a u-law WAV and an afconvert WAV with a 4 KB FLLR chunk (as a
temporary operator.wav, deleted after) all play.

## Home Assistant MQTT (2026-10-13, tested against Mosquitto on dualphone2)

Done: enable/disable from the card; 7 discovery messages valid JSON;
line state, attributes, RSSI and heap published; `cmd/ring/0` rang the
line; a test event arrived on `.../event`; 5 on/off toggles with no leak;
wrong password stays "connecting"; bad host refused with 400; 12 cycles of
wrong-password then restart with no crash (see below). Connected cost
about 21 KB of heap, +46 KB flash.

Found and fixed: esp-mqtt (IDF 5.2.8) writes `client->state` after
`esp_mqtt_client_stop()` returns, so destroying the client at once corrupts
whatever reuses the memory (crashed 1 time in 4 on stop-then-start while
retrying a refused login). `stop()` now waits 300 ms before destroying.
